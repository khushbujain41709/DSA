There are 4 major programming paradigms:
1) Brute Force
2) Dynamic programming
3) Greedy
4) Divide and Conquer

In brute force, we explore all possible outcomes, doesn't matter whether it leads to an answer or not.
There are two optimizations in brute force:
1) We prune irrelevant calls.
2) We trace the path for an answer and then we revert it for other possiblities.
